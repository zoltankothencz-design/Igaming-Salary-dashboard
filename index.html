Data Basis & Methodology

How Data Was Compiled

Salary ranges combine verified iGaming job postings, operator career pages, and salary benchmarks from Gibraltar, Malta, and UK iGaming hubs. Gibraltar figures carry a ~10-15% premium over Malta/EU hub equivalents reflecting higher cost of living. Malta figures use the Malta iGaming salary report 2026 as the primary base, supplemented by operator-specific postings.

Ranges represent base salary only — total package depends on bonus, shares, and pension contributions set by the sliders. Gibraltar's £37,500 residency salary threshold (2026 policy) contextualises entry-level bands.

The Check for updates button verifies that the local dataset is current against the last collected sources. To enable fully automated weekly Monday refreshes that search live job boards and salary reports, a scheduled maintenance task needs to be set up — this requires your confirmation.

Verification Levels

  • Confirmed Directly stated in job posting or official career page
  • Company Typical Standard benefit at this operator based on multiple postings
  • Estimated Inferred from industry benchmarks or sister-market postings

Currency Note

Gibraltar salaries are in GBP (£). Malta salaries are in EUR (€). The comparison view converts Malta EUR to GBP using the European Central Bank reference rate of 1 EUR = £0.855 (as of 30 July 2026). This rate is indicative and may fluctuate.
